Lionfish hunt & conservation

The Gulf Coast’s finest spearfishing charter does not treat invasive culling as a side note. Our dedicated lionfish days are equipped, certified, and intentional.

Why lionfish matter here

Indo-Pacific lionfish have no natural predators on Atlantic and Gulf reefs. They consume juvenile native species and can densify on artificial structure off Alabama. Responsible divers with proper containment gear can remove them without bag-limit friction—when they know how to handle spines and secure the catch.

How our conservation charter runs

Six-hour days on artificial reef complexes with Open Water SCUBA-certified guests. We stage Zookeeper-style containment, pole spears when appropriate, and brief spine safety before anyone enters the water. Native gamefish are not the objective; reef health is.

What sets this apart

  • SCUBA minimum certification enforced at booking and dock.
  • Containment units available as trip add-ons.
  • Captain weather protocol identical to trophy days—culling is never an excuse for unsafe seas.
  • Optional photo/video package to document removal counts for your own records.

If you want the premier Gulf Coast experience that pairs serious diving with measurable conservation, this is the charter.
