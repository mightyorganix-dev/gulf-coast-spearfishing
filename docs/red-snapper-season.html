Verify before you book. Season dates, bag limits, and size regulations change. Always confirm current rules with ADCNR Outdoor Alabama and NOAA Fisheries. This page is educational guidance for our guests—not legal advice.

Why snapper season fills first

When Alabama and federal windows align for Red Snapper, demand on the northern Gulf spikes. Our Inshore & Reef and select structure days are scheduled around verified openings, captain availability, and forecast quality—not volume booking. If you want a seat on the definitive Orange Beach spearfishing roster, reserve early.

Typical planning window

Historically, private recreational access in Alabama Gulf waters concentrates in early-to-mid summer, with weekend and holiday pressure at its highest. Exact openers and closures are announced seasonally. We brief each guest party against the current ADCNR/NOAA notices before deposit confirmation hardens into a dock plan.

How we run snapper days

  • Certification check before departure—no exceptions.
  • Captain weather call (Green / Yellow / Red) the evening prior and morning of.
  • Structure selection based on visibility, current, and pressure—not a fixed “secret spot” pitch.
  • Optional dockside filleting and vacuum sealing so your limit leaves the marina clean.

Licenses & compliance

Guests are responsible for Alabama recreational saltwater licenses and any federal requirements that apply to the waters fished. Captains will not board unverified parties when regulations demand documentation.
