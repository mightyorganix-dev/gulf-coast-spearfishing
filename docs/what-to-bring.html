What to bring

Arrive prepared. We handle the vessel, safety gear, and ice—you handle personal dive kit and comfort.

Pack these

  • Dive certification card (physical or digital)
  • Valid government ID
  • Alabama saltwater fishing license (as required)
  • Mask, snorkel, fins (or arrange rental in advance)
  • Wetsuit or rashguard suited to season
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, hat
  • Water, electrolytes, and light snacks
  • Soft cooler for fillets (leave in vehicle until return)
  • Dry bag for phone and wallet
  • Motion-sickness prevention if you are prone

We provide

  • USCG-compliant safety equipment
  • Oxygen kit and first-aid supplies
  • Ice and fish storage boxes
  • Speargun racks and dive platform access
  • Garmin navigation and sonar
  • Optional gear rentals selected at booking
  • Optional dockside cleaning & vacuum sealing
  • Captain weather updates on your itinerary

Arrival timing

Plan to be at the assigned marina slip 20 minutes before departure. Morning trips often leave at 06:00 or 07:00 AM; afternoon slots at 12:00 PM. Park in the charter customer lot and meet your captain at the slip listed on your itinerary.
