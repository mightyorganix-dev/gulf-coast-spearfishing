Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on pricing, party size, gear, certifications, weather, deposits, and dock etiquette.

Is pricing per person or per trip?

Charters are priced per trip, not per person. Inshore & Reef is $1,100 (5h), Offshore Oil Rig & Bluewater is $2,200 (9h), and Lionfish Hunt is $1,350 (6h). Gear add-ons may be per person or per trip as listed in booking.

How many guests can we bring?

Maximum six divers per vessel under USCG 6-pack licensing. Smaller parties are welcome; the trip price does not change with party size.

Do you provide spearfishing gear?

Safety equipment, ice, and fish boxes are included. Spearguns, floatlines, weight belts, lionfish containment, video packages, and dockside filleting are optional rentals available during booking.

Freediving vs SCUBA—what do you allow?

Both, depending on the trip. Inshore and offshore trips welcome qualified freedivers and SCUBA divers meeting the listed minimum cert. Lionfish conservation charters require Open Water SCUBA. Bring your physical or digital cert card.

What happens if weather is unsafe?

Captains make Green / Yellow / Red calls based on sea state, visibility, and current. Red cancellations are rescheduled; deposits transfer to the new date. Yellow means stand by for a final morning decision. Captain authority is final.

How do deposits work?

A 30% deposit holds your date. Balance is due 24 hours before dock time. Your deposit is processed securely at checkout; the remaining balance is collected before you leave the dock.

Is tipping expected?

Tips for captain and mate are customary when the day goes well—typically 15–20% of the charter total, handed dockside after fish are settled.

Do we need fishing licenses?

Yes. Alabama recreational saltwater licenses (and federal compliance for applicable waters/species) are the guest’s responsibility. Verify current requirements with ADCNR / NOAA before your trip.

Is alcohol allowed?

No alcohol during the charter. Spearfishing and boat operations demand full attention. Soft drinks and water are welcome; we recommend electrolytes for longer offshore days.
